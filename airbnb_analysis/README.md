## Airbnb Analysis Findings

#1 Market Concentration: Hosts managing 10 or more properties control 31.9% of Chicago's market, and the top 10% of all listings capture 36.1% of Columbus's total estimated revenue.

#2 The Superhost Premium: Superhosts successfully charge a higher median nightly price than regular hosts, including a $43.80 premium in Chicago.

#3 Extreme Seasonal Volatility: Historic monthly review data highlights severe seasonal swings in northern markets, with the Twin Cities seeing 2.37 times as much demand in its busiest month (August 2025) compared to its quietest.

#4 Cost-Effective Quality for Families: When applying strict quality constraints (4.8+ rating and 10+ reviews), Columbus's North Lindenx` neighborhood emerges as the region's best value for a family of four by offering the lowest median price per person.

#5 Missing Calendar Data: When matching the main listings dataset to the future availability calendar, Chicago had the highest number of properties missing from the calendar entirely.
